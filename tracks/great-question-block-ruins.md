Practice this one in offline VS mode. The track is known for its ending shortcut, which is quite technical, but can be very consistent. There’s also a grass shortcut at the beginning that is even bigger. The shock can be a little unpredictable here, so don't try to fish for a dodge. Instead the best way to protect yourself from the shock is to be near the front.
