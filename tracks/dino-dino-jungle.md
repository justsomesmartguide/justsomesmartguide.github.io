Going for either strat is a gamble. Sometimes shock happens and everyone who dodges wins, sometimes it doesn't happen and the runners and shroomers win. Regardless of whether you go for one or two items at the start, you'll want to take the shortcut in the indoor section on each lap. Keep in mind that since the 1.6.0 update, shock is a lot rarer and most likely won't happen on lap 2.
