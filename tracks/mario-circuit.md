Whatever you do here is a gamble, although knowing how to play this track can increase your chances of doing well. Use two mushrooms for the big section 2 shortcut, then use the third to take a shortcut afterwards, and then chain your star into the 2nd set on section 3.

The track itself is very short, make most of your comeback en route. There's a big shortcut after the second item set on the final lap, but that item set is not a good spot to use the shock just because it's so late.
