Pay attention to which direction the Peach film is moving in the film reel section. You’ll then either take the inside path or avoid it. There’s also an off-road shortcut at the beginning of the track. A comeback here is very difficult, so getting hit is especially punishing. The pack is also often really tight so it's super important to pay attention to your surroundings and have protection to make sure you don't get hit.
